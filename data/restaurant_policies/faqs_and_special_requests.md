# FAQs and Special Operational Requests

## 1. Modifying Placed Orders
- To add extra dishes to an order that is already `RECEIVED`, please contact support immediately within 3 minutes of ordering.
- If the order has reached `PREPARING` status, additional items must be placed as a separate order.

## 2. Eco-Friendly Cutlery Policy
- Cutlery, chopsticks, and extra paper napkins are provided only when explicitly selected by the customer during checkout to support environmental sustainability.

## 3. Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay.
- Cash on Delivery: Available in selected postcodes for orders under $40.00.

## 4. Large Group Bookings & Catering
- For catering orders of 10+ people or private dine-in bookings, requests must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance via our online catering portal or directly through restaurant support.

## 5. Promotion Codes and Vouchers
- Only one promo code or discount voucher can be applied per checkout transaction.
- Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively after an order has been submitted.
