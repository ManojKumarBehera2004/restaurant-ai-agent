# Delivery and Order Tracking Information

## 1. Delivery Timeframes & ETA
- **Standard Delivery:** Typical orders are prepared and delivered within **30 to 45 minutes** from the time the order is accepted.
- **Peak Hours & Extreme Weather:** During peak dinner hours (7:00 PM - 9:00 PM) or heavy weather conditions, delivery times may extend to **50 to 60 minutes**.
- **Live Tracking:** Customers can view the live progress of their order at each stage:
  1. `RECEIVED` - Order submitted, awaiting kitchen queue.
  2. `PREPARING` - Kitchen is actively preparing dishes.
  3. `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY` - Courier has picked up the food and is en route.
  4. `DELIVERED` - Package handed over to customer.

## 2. Courier Contact Information
- Courier phone numbers and live status are made available as soon as the status transitions to `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`.
- Couriers are instructed to attempt delivery up to 2 times and ring the door bell / phone the customer upon arrival.

## 3. Delivery Address Changes
- Delivery addresses can only be updated if the order is still in `RECEIVED` status.
- Once the kitchen begins `PREPARING` or the courier is dispatched, addresses cannot be modified to protect delivery partner routing.

## 4. Delivery Fees & Thresholds
- Standard delivery fee is **$3.50 to $4.99** depending on distance.
- **Free Delivery** is automatically applied for all food orders with a subtotal exceeding **$50.00**.
