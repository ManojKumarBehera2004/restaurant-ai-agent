# Restaurant Cancellation and Refund Policy

## 1. Order Cancellation Policy
- **Before Kitchen Acceptance (`RECEIVED` Status):** Customers can cancel their order free of charge with a 100% full refund if the cancellation request is made while the order is still in the `RECEIVED` status.
- **After Restaurant Acceptance & During Food Preparation (`PREPARING` Status):** Once the restaurant accepts the order and food preparation has begun, orders **CANNOT be cancelled** through self-service. Ingredients are already cooked specifically for the order. If an emergency cancellation is requested, a support ticket must be raised for managerial review.
- **Out for Delivery (`OUT_FOR_DELIVERY` Status):** Orders cannot be cancelled once they have left the restaurant with the delivery courier.
- **Delivered Orders (`DELIVERED` Status):** Cancellation is not applicable. For quality issues, see Section 3.

## 2. Refund Processing Guidelines
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** Bank refunds take **3 to 5 business days** depending on the customer's financial institution.
- **Digital Wallets (Apple Pay / Google Pay):** Processed within **24 to 48 hours**.
- **Instant Restaurant Store Credit:** Immediately credited to the customer account within **5 minutes** upon support approval.

## 3. Damaged, Cold, or Incorrect Orders
- If an order arrives damaged, spilled, or with missing items, customers must report the issue within **60 minutes** of delivery.
- Customer support will issue a full or partial refund or arrange an immediate replacement order.
- A photo of the delivered package may be requested by the support agent for verification.

## 4. Payment Deducted but Order Failed
- In rare cases where payment is deducted from the customer's bank account but the order shows as `FAILED` or did not generate an order confirmation, our payment gateway reconciles and auto-refunds within 24 hours.
- If the customer requires urgent verification, the support agent will immediately generate a High/Critical Priority Support Ticket to the billing team with the transaction details.
