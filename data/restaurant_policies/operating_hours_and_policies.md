# Operating Hours, Food Safety, and General Restaurant Policies

## 1. Operating Hours
- **Kitchen Hours:** Monday through Sunday: **10:00 AM to 11:30 PM** (Local Time).
- **Online Delivery Orders:** Cut-off time for placing same-day delivery orders is **11:15 PM**.
- **Holiday Hours:** Special operating hours are posted 48 hours in advance on our website and app.

## 2. Dietary & Allergen Policies
- **Allergens Handled:** Our kitchen processes peanuts, tree nuts, gluten/wheat, eggs, dairy, and shellfish.
- **Cross-Contamination Warning:** While we take strict measures to prevent cross-contamination, we cannot 100% guarantee an allergen-free environment for guests with severe airborne or contact anaphylaxis.
- **Customizations:** Gluten-free pizza crust and dairy-free cheese substitutes are available for select menu items upon request.

## 3. Food Hygiene & Quality Standards
- We hold a 5-Star Grade A Department of Health hygiene rating.
- Every delivery package is secured with a tamper-evident safety sticker. If the seal is broken upon receipt, customers should decline delivery and contact support.

## 4. Minimum Order Size
- The minimum order value for delivery is **$15.00** before taxes and delivery fees. There is no minimum spend for self-pickup / takeaway orders.
